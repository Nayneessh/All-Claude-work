# Monster Gains — friends edition

A training log + programming + progress app. This edition starts **empty**: no program, no routine, nothing pre-filled.
Choose exercises from the built-in library (or add your own), build your own program, or just tap **Quick workout** and log as you go.

* **File:** `MonsterGains-Friends-1.0.1.apk` (Android 7.0+). Checksum in `SHA256SUMS`; signer fingerprint in `signing-cert.sha256`.
* **Install:** open the file on your phone → allow *Install unknown apps* when asked → Install. It installs as its own app, separate from any other Monster Gains.
* **Your data:** saved on your phone first; works fully offline. To back it up and use it on a new phone, create an account (e-mail + password) from the *Back up* banner on the Today screen. Your data is private to your account.
* `test/` holds a debuggable twin of the same bundle used only by the automated emulator check.
